About Event
​AIBoomi Startup Weekend Kochi

​Build an AI product in 20 hours. Compete for $17,500 in OpenAI API credit pool.
Oct 23-24 | In person, Kochi | Free to participate

​What's up for grabs

​$17,500 OpenAI API credit pool for the top 3 teams

​Complimentary passes to AIBoomi's flagship event in Bengaluru (Mar 2027) for the top 3 teams

​Top 3 teams featured on Neysa's AI podcast

​4 weeks of mentor office hours after the weekend for the top 5 teams

​$100 OpenAI API credits for every participant, plus Neysa infra credits

​In partnership with
TinkerHub and Kerala Startup Mission (KSUM)

​How it works

​Apply solo or as a duo (max. 2 people)

​Submit your idea. Selected teams get an invite.

​Build from scratch in a 20-hour overnight buildathon, with founders and mentors on the floor

​Pitch to a jury judging for real-world utility and fundability

​Who should apply

​Aspiring and early-stage AI founders, product and tech folks, indie builders, domain experts, and students with a maker mindset. You don't need to be an AI expert. A bias for action is enough.

​AIBoomi is a SaaSBoomi community. AIBoomi Startup Weekend helps India's AI builders take the leap from sharp ideas to products, and eventually companies that matter.
