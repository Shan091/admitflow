## Demonstration

Show the problem briefly, then a Telegram student selecting course/batch, answering
qualification questions, correcting a fact and asking a grounded question. Show the
same persisted inquiry/conversation in the authenticated staff dashboard and the
human-request queue. Explain which facts/rules are institution-owned and where the
assistant abstains. Prepare for a five-minute desk review and a three-minute final
demo followed by two-minute Q&A, subject to organizer updates.

## Truthful claims

Separate existing modules from newly built/verified behavior. Disclose previous work
and the Meta restriction without claiming a WhatsApp journey that never succeeded.
Record the final tested build and language/evaluation limits. Do not fabricate revenue,
customers, conversion improvements, fluent-language performance or production readiness.
Prior-work permission, surprise requirement and portal details remain unresolved.
