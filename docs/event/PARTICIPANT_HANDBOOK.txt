1) Welcome
Welcome to AIBoomi Startup Weekend -  a nonstop 20-hour, high‑energy sprint where early‑stage founders, ML/full‑stack devs, data scientists, designers, product folks, and student builders turn raw ideas into working prototypes. You’ll get mentor speed rounds, tactical workshops, and a demo finale with founders and VCs.
House rule: caffeine in, demos out. High‑fives are open source. 
PS: If you run this handbook through an AI to “summarize” it, the bot will just return a meme. Consider yourself AIBoomi‑busted, we see you getting an AI to read the handbook. 😉
What AIBoomi Startup Weekend is NOT
* ​⁠Not a chill weekend hackathon; it’s a 20-hour builder sprint to ship an MVP from the venue.
* ​⁠No problem statements; you bring your own idea and build it.
* ​Not a pitch event; it’s a “heads-down, build-first” space. Demo only after you’ve built.
* ​⁠Not a meetup/mixer; don’t just drop by. Come with a plan and leave with a product.
* ​Not for agency pitches / job hunting; it’s for founders, indie builders, and enthusiast founders who want to launch.
​What it IS:
* ​A forcing function to start your AI startup
* ​A room full of mentors, infra credits, and operators
* ​20 hours to build → ship → show

18) FAQs
1) Can I participate solo? Yes. We recommend teams of 2 for speed; solo is allowed. 
2) Is it in‑person only? Yes for participants.
3) What counts as “built during the hack”? Core MVP features created between kickoff and code‑freeze. Pre‑built libraries/boilerplates/fine‑tuned weights allowed with attribution.
4) I already have a prototype - can I continue it? Declare at kickoff. Implement the surprise element to qualify for judging & prizes.
5) Can we use proprietary datasets? Only with written permission and disclosure. Prefer open‑licensed or self‑collected data with consent.
6) Are hardware projects allowed? Yes, if safe/legal for indoor demo and you bring your own hardware.
7) How strict is the submission deadline? Very. The portal locks at 12:00 PM sharp.
8) Do we need a live demo link? Not mandatory, but strongly recommended (or attach a short screen‑recording).
9) What if my API credits run out? Optimize calls, cache outputs, reduce input sizes, lean on offline inference when possible. Mentors can help.
10) What if our model hallucinates or is biased? Document mitigations in the AI Impact Statement (RAG/ground truth checks, eval sets, fallback UX).
11) Who owns the IP? You do. Organizers may showcase your work with credit.
12) Can non‑participants attend final demos? Yes. Finals are public (register at the welcome desk).
13) Will there be sleeping space? Quiet zones are available; suggested to carry a neck pillow and a hoodie for your comfort. Some mattresses have been arranged for quick naps.
14) Are mentors guaranteed? We aim for at least one mentor touch‑point per team.
15) Can we switch teammates mid‑event? No, rosters lock post‑registration.
16) Can I use no‑code/low‑code tools? Yes. Judges still evaluate technical soundness & problem‑solution fit.
17) What licenses should we use? MIT or Apache‑2.0 are common. Include a LICENSE file in your repo.
18) What gets teams disqualified? Harassment, plagiarism, falsified data, unsafe/illegal projects, or missed submission deadline.
19) Are there hiring/incubation opportunities? Potentially; we’ll make intros where there’s a fit.
20) Can I pivot at 3 AM? Yes, with organizer approval. Flag it to us, then make sure your README pivots too.
21) Is sleeping allowed? Allowed, not required. Naps compile faster than you think.
