How it works

From your question to a clear next step.

Start with a subject, a qualification level, or a question. AdmitFlow uses what you share to narrow the course list and keep the conversation on track.

01

Tell us what you want

Share a subject, study level, or goal. You can correct or change your preferences as you go.

02

Compare courses

See why a course matched, along with its entry requirements, study format, language support, and listed fee.

03

Ask about the details

Ask about courses and admissions. If a detail is missing from the information, AdmitFlow will say it cannot confirm it.

04

Choose what to do next

Review the requirements and fee before continuing. This build does not submit applications, and payment links use Razorpay test mode.
