How it works

A guided path, from first question to application.

Every answer that matters is computed in code, so the conversation stays consistent and honest.

01

Discover

Tell the bot what you are curious about, or ask it to help you explore. It remembers what you and your family mention.

02

Qualify

Eligibility is checked against sample rules in code, so the answer stays consistent and is never guessed by a model.

03

Ask

Admissions questions are answered from a fixed, fictional FAQ. When it is not sure, it says so instead of making something up.

04

Apply

If you qualify, it shares a test-mode application-fee link in rupees. No real payment is ever taken.

Built to be honest.

AdmitFlow is designed to say less, truthfully, rather than more.

Facts come from code

Eligibility and fees are computed from seeded, structured data, not written by the language model.

It fails closed

If search or indexing is unavailable, AdmitFlow stays quiet rather than answering with a guess.

Nothing here is real

The institution, courses, fees, and conversations are fictional samples built for a hackathon.
